## Our first model

- exact metric and strict submission validation;
- bounded-memory action aggregation;
- one-way chip-flow magnitude and directionality;
- per-player aggression/passivity residuals;
- behavior-specific hand signals;
- phase-quartile features for episodic coordination;
- table-group held-out validation;
- behavior-family prediction and legal shared-hand evidence ranking;
- cached intermediate parquet outputs and a schema-valid emergency submission.
